Meet me

Hello, I'm Elif

Dentistry · Final Year · Edirne

Dentistry calls for fine manual dexterity, a sound knowledge of anatomy and biomaterials, and a relationship of trust with the patient — all at once. I chose this field because it lets me see the results of my work directly and turn scientific thinking into practice with every case.

During the clinical phase of my five-year programme, I have worked chairside in every department of our faculty. Outside the clinic, I train regularly and enjoy bodybuilding; this discipline gives me a solid foundation for working ergonomically through long clinical sessions, staying focused and remaining consistent with my goals.

Diagnosis and planning matter as much as the procedure itself.
