About

Hello, I'm Elif

Dentistry calls for fine manual dexterity, a sound knowledge of anatomy and biomaterials, and a relationship of trust with the patient — all at once. I chose this field because it lets me see the results of my work directly and turn scientific thinking into practice with every case.

During the clinical phase of my five-year programme, I have worked chairside in every department of our faculty. Along the way I have learned that diagnosis and treatment planning matter as much as the procedure itself, and that a good outcome rests on the right indication, a meticulous working routine and the patient's active involvement in their own care.

Outside the clinic, I train regularly and enjoy bodybuilding. This discipline gives me a solid foundation for working ergonomically through long clinical sessions, staying focused and remaining consistent with the goals I set for myself.
